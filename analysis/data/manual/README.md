# Hand-checked data

Small facts that no machine-readable source provides cleanly. Each row was
checked against the club's honours list / contemporary reporting. Edit with care.

- `united_managers.csv`: United managers in the PL era. Start/end dates match
  Transfermarkt's coach history; `interim` and `how_it_ended` are hand-written.
  Carrick's permanent appointment (22 May 2026) was verified against ESPN's report.
- `united_trophies.csv`: major honours only (league, domestic cups, European
  and world club competitions). Community Shields are excluded. Dates are the
  date of the final, or roughly when the league title was sealed (league dates
  are approximate and only used to attribute the trophy). Trophies go to whoever
  was manager on that date.
